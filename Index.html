Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 5, 2026

Welcome to Dubai Property Buying Cost Calculator. We value your trust and are fully committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines our principles regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use our mobile application.

Information Collection and Use

Zero Personal Data Collection: This application does not collect, harvest, store, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, physical location, contact numbers, or financial account details.

All inputs entered into the calculator—including property purchase price, mortgage details, DLD percentages, and brokerage configurations—are processed exclusively and strictly on your device. Calculation logic operates locally in offline mode, and your financial estimations are never transmitted to external servers or cloud services.

Third-Party Services

To provide advertisement services and core system capabilities, our application integrates standard, trusted third-party SDKs provided by Google. These third-party services may automatically process non-personal diagnostic and advertising identifiers in accordance with their respective privacy policies:

  • Google Play Services: Used to maintain underlying Android platform services and ensure reliable application performance.
    Google Play Services Privacy Policy
  • Google AdMob: Used to display banner, interstitial, and optional rewarded advertisements. Google AdMob may use device identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID) and non-personal diagnostic metrics to serve contextual or personalized advertisements as governed by user consent and Google’s advertising terms.
    Google AdMob & Privacy Terms
  • Firebase Analytics & Crash Reporting (if enabled): May collect aggregated, anonymized app stability logs, operating system versions, and performance diagnostics to resolve crashes and improve user experience.
    Firebase Privacy & Security Documentation

Log Data, Cookies, and Device Permissions

Log Data: When an unexpected error or crash occurs, automated error diagnostics (such as device manufacturer, Android OS version, and stack traces) may be collected anonymously via Google Play Services to aid in debugging and maintenance.

Cookies & Tracking: The application itself does not employ cookies. However, third-party advertising SDKs (such as AdMob) may utilize industry-standard device identifiers for ad measurement and frequency capping.

Device Permissions:

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required strictly to load AdMob advertisement units and retrieve updated ad network parameters.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to verify active connectivity before requesting network-based ad units.
  • Local File Creation / Sharing: When you generate a PDF cost breakdown report, it is created locally within your device's sandbox. Sharing or printing the PDF uses standard Android system intents directly controlled by you.

Data Security & Retention

Because the app does not collect personal user data or host remote user databases, there is no transmission or storage of your personal financial calculations on external servers. All temporary calculator inputs and generated PDF files remain locally in the application's secure sandbox on your device and are removed when the app cache is cleared or when the application is uninstalled.

Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

It is not directed at or marketed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly solicit, collect, or retain any personal information from children under 13 years of age. If you believe a child has provided personal information via our application or integrated services, please contact us immediately so we can take appropriate measures.
